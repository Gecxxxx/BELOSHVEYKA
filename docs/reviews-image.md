# Иллюстрация для отзывов на главной

Создано встроенным ImageGen. Концептуальная предметная фотография деталей рабочей куртки; не фотография клиентского заказа. Подпись на сайте: «ИИ-иллюстрация». Другие страницы отзывов сохраняют свои реальные фотографии.

Файлы: `public/assets/concept/reviews-detail.webp` (1000 × 1000), `public/assets/concept/reviews-detail-small.webp` (500 × 500). WebP 82/78. Оригинал PNG сохранён отдельно без изменений.

Промпт:

Use case: product-mockup. Create a square premium editorial still-life photograph for the customer reviews section of a Russian workwear sewing company website. A carefully folded graphite charcoal cotton-twill work jacket, close view of a beautifully constructed patch pocket, neat double stitching and a matte dark snap button, small glimpse of muted warm ochre inner facing. Garment fills lower two thirds of composition diagonally, with softly draped folds. Warm off-white plaster tabletop, natural window light from upper left, delicate realistic shadows, tactile cloth weave. Palette warm ivory, charcoal, tiny muted ochre accent. Understated sophisticated industrial workwear brand, calm and natural, photographic and not CGI. Single cohesive garment, no people, no hands, no mannequin, no tools, no cards, no labels, no visible text, no logo, no watermark, no collage. This is a conceptual illustration of garment craftsmanship, not a documented client order. Output only the photograph, not a website or UI.
